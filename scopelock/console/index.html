How it works

One call in, one call out, one policy in the middle.

Two Guava agents, one Python object arbitrating whose turn it is. Translation gives someone words. This gives them representation.

1

She calls in once, in Spanish

The agent takes the case, reads the mandate back in plain Spanish, and only a clear "sí" confirms it. Then she can hang up.

2

The institution calls the number she was given

A scripted AI disclosure plays first, before any model-driven turn — never hidden, never implied.

3

Every action crosses a hard policy gate

Allowed, holder-only, or forbidden. Forbidden verbs like agree_payment and disclose_ssn have no executable handler — there is no prompt to jailbreak.

4

Consequential asks go back to her, live

The agent parks the institution leg, calls her back automatically, asks in plain Spanish, and relays her answer in English.

Honest, on purpose

What this is not

  • Spanish, French, German, and Italian only — Vietnamese and Cantonese families are not served yet.
  • Authentication is unsolved where an institution demands the holder speak specific answers herself.
  • Never legal or medical advice. It gets a human decision to the phone, not a verdict.
